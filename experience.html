Domain Expertise

My professional engagements are architected around three core pillars. For over three decades, I have deployed these competencies to salvage distressed capital programs, establish rigorous financial governance, and bridge the perilous gap between executive vision and operational reality.

Finance Transformation & Modernization

The transition from legacy cash-based accounting to modern, accrual-based financial frameworks is fraught with operational risk. I specialize in orchestrating these massive paradigm shifts for government and mega-corporate entities. By leading IPSAS (International Public Sector Accounting Standards) first-time adoptions, I ensure that financial transparency meets international scrutiny.

  • IPSAS Adoption: End-to-end management of accrual accounting transitions in public sectors.
  • ERP Alignment: Translating C-suite business requirements into actionable Oracle/SAP technical architectures.
  • Process Redesign: Eliminating systemic bottlenecks in capital expenditure cycles.
  • Change Management: Cultivating a culture of financial accountability across non-financial engineering teams.

Strategic Planning & Project Financial Controls

Managing multi-billion-dollar infrastructure portfolios requires far more than basic budgeting; it demands predictive financial modeling and rigorous commercial controls. I have engineered integrated five-year forecasts and capital deployment frameworks for projects that reshaped the skylines and coastlines of the UAE and Qatar.

  • Zero-Based Budgeting: Establishing absolute cost justification for massive capital outlays.
  • Cash Flow Forecasting: Advanced predictive modeling to prevent liquidity crunches in offshore mega-projects.
  • Commercial Costing: Bridging the gap between engineering estimates and actual financial drawdowns.
  • Variance Analysis: Implementing strict tolerance thresholds for contractor milestones.

Governance, Risk & Decision Support

True executive leadership relies on unimpeachable data and robust risk management. Drawing upon my foundational years in forensic auditing at Arthur Andersen, I design internal control environments that protect organizational integrity while enabling, rather than hindering, rapid scale.

  • Board-Level Reporting: Distilling complex operational data into actionable C-suite financial narratives.
  • Internal Controls: Architecting fraud-resistant, compliant workflows across decentralized operations.
  • Policy Development: Writing the operational rulebooks that govern organizational finance.
  • Executive Mentorship: Coaching operational directors to understand the financial implications of their decisions.
