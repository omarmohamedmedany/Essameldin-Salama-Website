Professional Experience

My professional engagements are architected around three core pillars. For over three decades, I have deployed these competencies to salvage distressed capital programs, establish rigorous financial governance, and bridge the perilous gap between executive vision and operational reality.

Finance Transformation & Modernization

I lead and advise complex finance-transformation programmes that connect executive priorities with governance, processes, people, data, and enabling technology. My experience includes Technical PMO leadership for Ashghal’s wider finance transformation, supporting Qatar’s pioneering government work in IPSAS-based accrual accounting, infrastructure fixed-asset registers, government costing, executive visualization, and data analytics for decision-making.

  • Public-Sector Finance Transformation: Technical PMO leadership supporting the transition from legacy cash-based reporting toward IPSAS-aligned accrual practices and stronger financial governance.
  • ERP & Systems Enablement: 25 years of extensive super-user and Technical PMO implementation experience, translating executive and business requirements into practical Oracle and SAP-enabled solutions.
  • Process & Operating-Model Redesign: Aligning policies, controls, workflows, roles, and systems across the end-to-end finance and asset lifecycle.
  • Change Leadership & Capability Transfer: Building ownership, accountability, and financial understanding across finance, engineering, operational, and technology teams.

Strategic Planning & Project Financial Controls

Mega-project leadership requires more than budgeting. It requires disciplined commercial judgment, long-range planning, reliable cash-flow visibility, and controls that connect engineering progress with financial outcomes. Across infrastructure, ports, marine operations, and major development portfolios, I have provided financial leadership and control over cumulative project exposure exceeding US$100 billion across approximately 20 years.

  • Strategic Planning & Budgeting: Developing integrated business plans, multi-year budgets, forecasts, and capital-deployment frameworks.
  • Cash-Flow & Funding Visibility: Forecasting project and portfolio requirements to support timely executive and Board decisions.
  • Project Financial & Commercial Controls: Connecting scope, progress, commitments, contracts, costs, risks, and forecast outcomes.
  • Performance & Variance Management: Converting operational and financial data into early warnings, corrective actions, and accountable performance discussions.

Governance, Risk & Decision Support

Executives need trusted information, proportionate controls, and advice that supports decisions rather than slows them down. Drawing on my Big Four audit and advisory foundation and subsequent executive finance roles, I help organizations strengthen governance, risk management, policy frameworks, internal controls, performance management, and executive decision support.

  • Executive & Board Advisory: Translating complex financial, commercial, operational, and transformation matters into clear choices, risks, and recommended actions.
  • Governance, Risk & Internal Control: Designing practical policies, delegations, controls, and assurance arrangements suited to the organization’s operating reality.
  • Data, Analytics & Visualization: Establishing decision-support capabilities that convert fragmented information into reliable management insight.
  • Executive Mentoring & Practitioner Coaching: Guiding executives, managers, finance professionals, transformation teams, and emerging leaders through real organizational challenges, career development, and implementation.
